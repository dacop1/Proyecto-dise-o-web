A building with memory

Before it was a table, it was a granary. This is the story of the Pósito de Labradores and how it brings the village together again.

WHAT IS A PÓSITO

Everyone's granary

Pósitos were public granaries where farmers kept the grain. It secured the village's food in years of poor harvest and was lent out as seed for the next sowing.

Save today so that no one goes hungry tomorrow.

THE BUILDING

The Pósito de Labradores of Prado del Rey

The Pósito de Labradores is one of the most memory-filled buildings in Prado del Rey. For generations its walls held the grain of those who worked the land of the sierra.

TIMELINE

From granary to table

  1. Origin: the village builds the pósito to store the common grain.
  2. Centuries of use: farmers use it to store their harvest and borrow seed.
  3. Change of use: the building stops serving as a granary.
  4. Today: it is restored as a restaurant-bar, a meeting place around the table.
